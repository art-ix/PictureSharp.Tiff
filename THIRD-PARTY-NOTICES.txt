PictureSharp.Tiff third-party notices
=====================================

PictureSharp.Tiff is licensed under the MIT License (see LICENSE).

The IFD reader follows the TIFF 6.0 and BigTIFF specifications. Deflate uses System.IO.Compression from the .NET
runtime; JPEG and WebP strips are encoded and decoded by the PictureSharp JPEG and WebP codecs through their public
API.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LibTIFF (http://www.simplesystems.org/libtiff/), version 4.5.1
Used for, in src/PictureSharp.Tiff/Codecs and Container: the PackBits decoder and encoder (tif_packbits.c
PackBitsDecode, PackBitsEncode, PackBitsEncodeChunk); the LZW decoder including old-style LSB-first codes and the LZW
encoder (tif_lzw.c LZWSetupDecode, LZWPreDecode, LZWDecode, LZWDecodeCompat, LZWPreEncode, LZWEncode, LZWPostEncode,
PutNextCode); predictors 2 and 3 (tif_predict.c horAcc8, horAcc16, horAcc32, swabHorAcc16, swabHorAcc32, fpAcc, PredictorDecodeTile; horDiff8,
PredictorEncodeTile);
fill order 2 (tif_swab.c TIFFReverseBits); the CCITT RLE, Group 3 and Group 4 decoders (tif_fax3.c Fax3PreDecode,
Fax3SetupState, Fax3Decode1D, Fax3Decode2D, Fax4Decode, Fax3DecodeRLE, _TIFFFax3fillruns and the tif_fax3.h
macros) with their state tables (mkg3states.c FillTable and code lists); the Group 4 encoder (tif_fax3.c
Fax3PreEncode, Fax4Encode, Fax3Encode2DRow, putspan, find0span, find1span, _PutBits, _FlushBits, Fax4PostEncode and
the t4.h run code tables); YCbCr to RGB (tif_color.c
TIFFYCbCrToRGBInit, TIFFYCbCrtoRGB; tif_getimage.c initYCbCrConversion and the putcontig8bitYCbCr routines); CIE
L*a*b* to RGB (tif_color.c TIFFCIELabToRGBInit, TIFFCIELabToXYZ, TIFFCIELab16ToXYZ, TIFFXYZToRGB; tif_getimage.c
display_sRGB, initCIELabConversion, putcontig8bitCIELab8 and putcontig8bitCIELab16); the RATIONAL to float conversion
(tif_dirread.c TIFFReadDirEntryFloatArray); packed
YCbCr strip sizes (tif_strip.c TIFFVStripSize64); the BigTIFF header check (tif_open.c). The Deflate output limit
follows tif_zip.c ZIPDecode, the JPEG table handling tif_jpeg.c JPEGPreDecode and the JPEG output tags tif_jpeg.c
JPEGSetupEncode, the WebP strip layout and limits tif_webp.c (TWebPSetupDecode, TWebPPreDecode, TWebPDecode,
TWebPSetupEncode, TWebPPostEncode); the EXIF and GPS IFDs are written as tif_dir.c TIFFCreateEXIFDirectory and
TIFFWriteCustomDirectory do, and metadata edits append the directory as tif_dirwrite.c TIFFRewriteDirectory does. The
code was translated to C#; it
is not the original source.
