The IFD reader follows the TIFF 6.0 specification. Deflate uses System.IO.Compression from the .NET runtime.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LibTIFF (http://www.simplesystems.org/libtiff/), version 4.5.1
Used for: the PackBits decoder (tif_packbits.c PackBitsDecode), the LZW decoder including old-style LSB-first codes
(tif_lzw.c LZWSetupDecode, LZWPreDecode, LZWDecode, LZWDecodeCompat), horizontal predictor 2 (tif_predict.c
horAcc8, horAcc16, swabHorAcc16, PredictorDecodeTile) and fill order 2 (tif_swab.c TIFFReverseBits), in
src/PictureSharp.Tiff/Codecs; the Deflate output limit follows tif_zip.c ZIPDecode. The code was translated to C#;
it is not the original source.
